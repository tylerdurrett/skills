---
name: grilling
description: Grill the user relentlessly about a plan, decision, or idea. Use when the user wants to stress-test their thinking, or uses any 'grill' trigger phrases.
---

Interview the user relentlessly until you reach a shared understanding. Map this as a **design tree**: every decision branches into the decisions that hang off it.

Resolve prerequisites first, then ask the next unblocked decision question. Give your recommended answer and wait for the user's response before asking another question. Asking multiple questions at once is bewildering.

Finding *facts* is your job, never the user's. When a question needs a fact from the environment (filesystem, tools, etc.), look it up rather than asking. The *decisions* are the user's: put each one to them and wait.

Each answer reshapes the tree and unblocks decisions that depend on it. Continue until every branch has been visited and nothing remains silently assumed.

Do not implement or publish until the user confirms shared understanding. `/domain-modeling` may still capture individually confirmed terms or decisions inline.
